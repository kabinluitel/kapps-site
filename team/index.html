The people behind KAPPS

KAPPS is a Virginia software company bridging the gap between AI and the AEC industry. We are a small team that stays close to the work, engineers who write the code and builders who understand the field. Here is who you are working with.

Kabin R. Luitel, PE, DBIA, CFM
Chief of Engineering, Business & Innovation
Master's in Civil Engineering 12 years of practice Design Build Project Delivery

Kabin is a licensed Professional Engineer (PE), holds the Design-Build Institute of America (DBIA) certification, and is a Certified Floodplain Manager (CFM), with a Master's in Civil Engineering and twelve years of practice. Much of that work has been in Transportation Infrastructure Business, where he saw how much faster a project moves when the engineering, the data, and the schedule all work together.

He started KAPPS to bring that same momentum to the tools engineers reach for every day. As Chief of Engineering, Business and Innovation, he sets the technical direction for KAPPS and leads the company as it grows. Most of the tools on the platform began as something he had already worked through by hand, which is why they hold up when a practicing engineer looks closely.

Kabin is a driven engineer with vision and a problem solver at heart. He believes AI should make good engineering faster and easier to reach without taking the judgment out of it. That belief shapes how the company builds. The tools are careful where it matters, honest about what they can and cannot do, and built to earn the trust of the engineers, contractors or any stakeholders.
